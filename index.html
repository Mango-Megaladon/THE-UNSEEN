FEATURES

What makes it move different

As a gorilla VR game, The Unseen stands apart from other backrooms horror games by making how you move the whole point.

  • →
    Real push-based locomotion
    Get your hands near a surface and push off — real physics, real momentum, no snap-turning your way to safety.
  • →
    A growing roster of monsters
    Starting with The Watcher — more to come as the game grows.
  • →
    Backrooms that don't stay still
    Liminal, fluorescent-lit corridors that never feel quite safe to be in.
  • →
    Built by Viper Studios
    An independent project, built from scratch and shaped by community feedback.

Policies & Rules

  • —
    Roomscale or standing play, controllers required.

    Contains flashing lights, jump scares, and unsettling imagery.
  • —
    Movement is push-based — clear space around you before playing.
  • —
    Take a break if you feel dizzy or disoriented.
