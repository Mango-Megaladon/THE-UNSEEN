HOW IT'S DIFFERENT

Not quite either genre

Most VR movement games are built to play, not to scare you. Most VR horror games use joysticks or teleporting, not your actual body. THE UNSEEN sits between the two on purpose — the fear hits harder because escaping it takes real physical effort, not a thumbstick push.

WHAT IS THIS PLACE

Nobody built this

No one designed it. It doesn't appear on any blueprint, any map, any deed. You don't find the backrooms — it finds you. A wrong turn. A door that wasn't there yesterday. A moment of not paying attention.

The walls are always the same sick yellow. The lights always hum the same broken frequency. And something that used to be alive is always a few rooms ahead of you, or a few rooms behind — you're never quite sure which is worse.

Nobody has mapped it end to end. Nobody who's tried has come back to finish the map.

BOILERPLATE

THE UNSEEN is a VR-only backrooms horror game in development by Viper Studios, built around real push-based gorilla movement instead of joystick or teleport locomotion. Players are hunted through shifting liminal corridors by The Watcher, a monster that's half organic, half machine.

KEY FACTS
  • Platform: Meta Quest (VR only)
  • Genre: Horror / Movement
  • Status: In development, pre-release
  • Studio: Viper Studios
  • Players: Single-player
